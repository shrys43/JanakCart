## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-velocity, reliable e-commerce marketplace experience tailored specifically for digital commerce in Nepal. The design direction merges the structured, information-dense ergonomics of major South Asian retail leaders (such as Flipkart) with an authentic, culturally anchored Nepali tone.

The aesthetic follows **Modern High-Density Retail with Tactile Tonal Layers**. It balances visual urgency—essential for festive shopping, flash discounts, and daily deals—with institutional trust, vital for overcoming online transaction hesitancy. Key emotional pillars are:
- **Festive Vitality:** Celebrating shopping events (Dashain, Tihar, New Year Dhamaka) through high-energy color cues.
- **Uncompromising Trust:** Rock-solid payment, verification, and logistics clarity (fonepay, eSewa, Khalti, COD, and Valley 24h delivery assurances).
- **Functional Density:** Scannable, modular product grids and tabular deal comparisons that serve users across varying bandwidths and devices.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around high utility, cultural resonance, and clear cognitive signals:

- **Primary (`#C8102E` - Himalayan Crimson):** Evoking Nepal’s national crimson, this color acts as the primary transactional driver for Add to Cart, Buy Now, urgent price cuts, and high-impact hero promotional banners.
- **Secondary (`#0F2444` - Royal Navy):** Provides structural grounding. Applied to primary search headers, main utility navigation, footer surfaces, and verification accents to project enterprise durability and secure infrastructure.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B` - Festive Amber):** Reserved for star ratings, "Dhamaka" sale chips, countdown meters, and high-priority promotional alerts.
- **Semantic Indicators:**
  - *Trust / In-Stock:* `#10B981` (Emerald Green) signifies JanakCart Assured badges, successful transactions, stock availability, and total savings readouts.
  - *Base Surface:* `#F8FAFC` (Slate Off-White) provides neutral canvas backing to reduce glare while keeping product photography front-and-center.
  - *Card Surface:* `#FFFFFF` (Pristine White) creates strict isolation for individual product nodes.
  - *Border Subtle:* `#E2E8F0` defines container boundaries cleanly without visual noise.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy uses **Plus Jakarta Sans**, balancing contemporary geometric design with excellent legibility down to dense, tabular sizes.

- **Devanagari Harmonization:** When switching to or displaying bilingual Nepali copy (नेपाली), fallback font stacks must prioritize systemic Unicode weights (`system-ui`, `Mukta`, or `Noto Sans Devanagari`) to preserve alignment across baseline heights.
- **Price Architecture:** E-commerce pricing uses tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`). The currency sign (`रू` or `NPR`) aligns with the base numeric size, maintaining strict hierarchy over struck-through original MRP values.
- **Dense Scannability:** Micro labels (`label-sm`, `11px`) use bold weights and subtle positive tracking for badges like "FREE DELIVERY" or "DHAMAKA DEAL".

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a hybrid **Dense Grid Framework** optimized for rapid browsing and visual scanning across varied devices:

- **Breakpoints:**
  - `Mobile (< 640px)`: 4-column layout, 8px gutters, 12px margins. Product feeds render primarily as a compact 2-column card grid or horizontal scrolling rails.
  - `Tablet (640px - 1024px)`: 8-column layout, 16px gutters, 20px margins.
  - `Desktop (> 1024px)`: 12-column fixed container (max-width: 1280px) centered with 16px gutters and 24px outer margins, allowing dense 4-column to 6-column product layouts.
- **Rhythm & Padding:** Component interiors rely strictly on the `0.25rem` (4px) scale: 8px for internal badge padding, 12px–16px for card bodies, and 24px–32px for promotional section separation.

## Elevation & Depth

To maintain high data density without visual clutter, this design system uses **Layered Surfaces with Diffused Micro-Shadows** paired with crisp outline definitions:

- **Level 0 (Base / Flat):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces on `#F8FAFC` canvas framed by a 1px solid `#E2E8F0` border. Applied to standard static product cards, category carousels, and specification tables.
- **Level 1 (Card Hover / Floating Item):** Box shadow `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 36, 68, 0.08), 0 2px 4px -1px rgba(15, 36, 68, 0.04)`. Secondary navy undertones preserve brand tinting over generic gray shadows.
- **Level 2 (Sticky Navigations & Drawers):** Box shadow `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(15, 36, 68, 0.12)`. Applied to sticky cart checkout footers on mobile and main search app-bars.
- **Level 3 (Modals / Pincode Selectors):** Box shadow `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(15, 36, 68, 0.18)` over a 40% opacity navy backdrop blur.

## Shapes

The design system operates at **Level 2 (Rounded)**:
- Standard UI nodes (Inputs, Base Buttons, Deal Chips) use `0.5rem` (8px).
- Product Cards and Modal Containers use `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px) or `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / 24px) to ensure soft, accessible perimeters.
- Micro tags ("JanakCart Assured", Discount Ribbons) leverage directional corners (e.g., top-left `0.5rem`, bottom-right `0.5rem`) to echo modern marketplace badge patterns.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Buy Now / Checkout):** Deep Himalayan Crimson background (`#C8102E`), crisp white text, bold label style, 12px vertical padding, subtle interactive scale feedback (`0.98` on click).
- **Secondary Action (Add to Cart):** Royal Navy (`#0F2444`) with white typography, or amber highlight (`#F59E0B`) with dark navy label during flash sales.
- **Tertiary / Utility:** Pure white surface, 1px border (`#CBD5E1`), navy text, 8px corner radius.

### Product Cards
- **Structure:** Crisp white background with a 1px border (`#E2E8F0`). Product imagery is presented on a neutral square canvas (1:1 aspect ratio) with a subtle inset border.
- **Information Hierarchy:**
  1. Top-left corner: Floating discount badge (`35% OFF` in Himalayan Crimson pill).
  2. Top-right corner: Heart / Wishlist icon button with micro-elevation.
  3. Body: Category micro-label, 2-line truncated title (`title-md`), star rating pill (Amber `#F59E0B` icon + review count).
  4. Footer: Bold current price in `price-display` paired with struck-through MRP, followed by the emerald "JanakCart Assured" tick.

### Delivery & Trust Badges
- **Kathmandu Valley Express:** Compact pill with emerald green background tint (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.1)`), emerald text, and a localized delivery van icon ("24hr Valley Delivery").
- **Payment Method Badges:** Clean outline container badges showing localized payment partners (eSewa, Khalti, Fonepay, Cash on Delivery) rendered with consistent horizontal heights (20px) to boost checkout trust.

### Form Inputs & Search Bar
- **Global Search:** Distinct high-contrast Royal Navy border on active focus with an integrated primary crimson "Search" button on desktop. Embedded pincode/location selector prefix ("Deliver to: Kathmandu...").
- **Form Controls:** Checkboxes and radios use a 2px Crimson outline with a solid `#C8102E` fill upon selection.
